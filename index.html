The Three-Body Problem

Liu Cixin

Liu Cixin's Hugo-winning first-contact epic opens in China's Cultural Revolution, where a young astrophysicist's despair leads her to invite an alien civilization to Earth — and follows the fallout forty years later, when a physicist starts seeing a countdown only he can see. Hard science, deep grief, and the most vertigo-inducing ideas in modern science fiction.

Episode 13

A Gentleman in Moscow

Amor Towles

Amor Towles's 2016 novel traps Count Alexander Rostov — a Russian aristocrat sentenced in 1922 to lifelong house arrest inside Moscow's Metropol Hotel — and asks what remains when everything that defined a person is taken away. Across three decades of purges and war, the Count learns that freedom is not the absence of constraint but what you choose to do with it.

Episode 12

The Forty Rules of Love

Elif Shafak

Elif Shafak's million-copy bestseller braids two love stories eight centuries apart: a bored Massachusetts housewife reading her way toward a new life, and the thirteenth-century friendship between the mystic poet Rumi and the wandering dervish Shams of Tabriz. Between them run forty rules of love — a spiritual operating system for dismantling the ego, one small death at a time.
